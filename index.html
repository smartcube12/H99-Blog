Hi there! This is My most recent post!

4/28/25

We just finished our second to last homework grading. The one week grading deadline was a bit of a doozy but I can understand it since this homework used to be much longer in the past. The split makes this one all about algorithms and will likely make the last homework all about recursion and other such equations. It feels weird to see these things change right before my eyes but learning to be flexible about it makes it so much easier to adapt. Since I will be busy with finals the next post won’t be until after I finish this experience, so I wanted to take a bit to reflect on it. I think my biggest take aways from being a TA is showing up and being adaptable. Just being around to help even a little bit makes things easier for the students and even if I couldn’t help, I could lead them to who could. Just the other day I got an email from a student about extra credit points not being counted and although I couldn’t do anything I was able to get him in contact with the TA that graded his work, and he got his proper credit. I was already fairly adaptable beforehand being a musician in high school, but teaching requires a whole other level especially tutoring. Each student learns differently and it’s your job to figure out how best to teach them, so they understand the content. I was never a very diagram heavy person but now I could whip up a visual representation in no time. Being able to adapt to unforeseen situations is a vital skill and I’m thankful that my time as a TA was able to improve upon it.
